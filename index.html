Heat pump systems

Three parts, one system

We fit a lot of renewable energy here in Guernsey. A typical heat pump job takes in the outdoor unit, a hot water cylinder, the controls, and the heating side, underfloor or radiators. We often convert an old oil or electric system at the same time.

Why Datum

Done right, then looked after

Fitted to the manufacturer’s standard

The manufacturer of one underfloor system told us ours was one of the best installs they had ever seen, and now uses our photos as an example of how to lay it correctly.

Practising what we fit

We fit a lot of renewable energy in Guernsey, so we’re upgrading our own fleet to electric vehicles and phasing out the old diesels to support Guernsey’s carbon neutral aim.

We stay on the job

Servicing, filters and flushes as well as new installs. On one busy day we serviced five cylinders, with nine more booked for the next.

Big jobs, small jobs

From oil boilers, heat pumps and solar to new builds and tap washers. If it’s plumbing, heating or renewables, give us a call.
